About

Plant science across experiments and data.

I am a scientist passionate about genetics. Originally from Grenoble, France, I studied molecular biology at Université Grenoble Alpes before moving to Germany for a PhD in Genetics at the University of Cologne. I then spent eight years as a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Plant Breeding Research, investigating the genetic and environmental factors underlying complex trait variation in plants. My work has taken me from molecular biology to digital phenotyping, genomics, quantitative genetics, and machine learning. I am now back in the Alps, in beautiful Lucerne, Switzerland.
